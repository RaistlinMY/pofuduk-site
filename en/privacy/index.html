Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What you write in Pofuduk never reaches us. No account, no server: your goals, mood check-ins and notes are stored only on your phone. An ad is shown only when you ask for one, for a reward, and it is never personalised. To improve the app we collect only nameless usage counts and crash reports; you can turn this off in Settings.

Pofuduk is made by My Point Bilişim ve Danışmanlık Sanayi ve Ticaret Limited Şirketi ("we" here). Address: DAP Royal Center Sitesi A Blok, Altayçeşme Mah. Çamlı Sok. No: 16/38, 34843 Maltepe, İstanbul, Türkiye. Email: info@mypoint.com.tr.

What the app stores

While you use Pofuduk, the following is stored on your phone:

  • the name you gave Pofuduk, your own name if you shared it, and the support areas you picked when you met (e.g. stress, sleep, study),
  • your goals: their names, which days or how many times a week (or a day) they repeat, reminder times, and the days you did, skipped or snoozed them,
  • your mood check-ins and the notes you write,
  • Keyif, Mandalina, Pofuduk's trips and postcards,
  • your notification settings and times.

All of it stays in the Pofuduk app's own space on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else; we cannot see it or access it.

Your mood check-ins and notes

Mood check-ins and notes may count as sensitive information about your health. That's why they never leave your phone and are never sent out for any processing; the summaries on the Insights screen are worked out on your phone too.

If a feature like backup or sharing with friends is added later, your mood check-ins and notes will not leave your phone without your explicit permission.

Widgets and the Lock Screen

For its widgets, Pofuduk writes Pofuduk's name and picture, your streak, the Keyif bar, how many goals you have today and, for the home screen widget, the names of your open goals into a space on your phone that the app and its widgets share. This doesn't leave your phone either. The Lock Screen shows only counts, never goal names; still, anyone who sees your home screen can read the goals on the widget. Remove the widget if you'd rather they didn't.

While Settings → "Pofuduk with you all day" is on, until your goals are done the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show Pofuduk, how many goals are left, the Keyif bar and, on a trip, the time until it's back; never goal names. This is put together on your phone and doesn't leave it. You can turn it off in the same place.

Location

Pofuduk doesn't ask for your location. To turn the room from day to night it uses your phone's time zone (e.g. Europe/London) and works out sunset for that time zone's city on your phone. This doesn't leave your phone either.

Your phone's backups

If backups are on (iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), Pofuduk's data goes into them like any other app's, so it isn't lost when you change phones. Backups are kept in your Apple or Google account under those companies' terms; we have no access to them. Pofuduk uses no other cloud sync.

Permissions

Pofuduk only asks for notifications: the morning hello, the evening "how are you?", goal reminders and Pofuduk coming back from a trip. They are scheduled on your phone, not sent over the internet. Without the permission the rest of the app works the same.

Exporting

Settings → "Export my data" opens all your records, notes included, as a readable file in your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes; wherever you send it, that place's rules apply. The temporary copy the app makes is deleted when the share sheet closes.

Ads and tracking

Pofuduk shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a reward (a trip back sooner, a few tangerines); never on goals, moods, breathing, help or onboarding. Ads come from Google AdMob and are requested non-personalised: we don't track you across apps or use your phone's advertising identifier. To show an ad and prevent fraud, AdMob processes technical data such as device type, operating system and IP address; in Europe, your consent is asked first. What you write, your goals and your moods are never sent to the ad service.

Usage counts and crash reports

To fix and improve Pofuduk we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They send, without your name:

  • which screens are opened and for how long the app is used,
  • that something happened, such as a goal added or done, a mood logged, a breathing or focus session finished, or something bought in the Bazaar (never what it contained),
  • technical details of a crash or error,
  • device model, operating system and app version, country (roughly, from the IP address), language and a random app-install ID.

Your goal names, mood levels, notes, Pofuduk's name and your name are never sent. No advertising ID is collected; this data is not used for ads or to track you across apps, and it is not shared with Google for advertising. It is processed on Google's servers and kept for at most 14 months. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Data → Anonymous usage data; once off, nothing is sent.

Third parties

Pofuduk uses three third-party services: Google AdMob for optional ads, Google Firebase for nameless usage counts and crash reports (both above), and RevenueCat for the Pofuduk+ subscription. RevenueCat processes only a random, nameless ID and the store's purchase record, to know whether you are subscribed; payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play and your card details never reach us. On the help screen, call buttons open your phone's dialer and links open your browser; the line you call and the site you open have their own rules. The app is downloaded from the App Store and Google Play, whose own privacy policies apply.

If you email us

If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply to you and for nothing else. You can also write to info@mypoint.com.tr to use your rights under data protection law, such as article 11 of Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data.

Deleting your data

You can delete goals one by one from the goal screen, and mood check-ins by pressing and holding them in the calendar. To remove everything, use Settings → "Delete all my data" or uninstall the app. Copies may remain in phone backups made earlier; you can delete those in your Apple or Google account's backup settings.

Changes

If a feature that changes how your data is handled is added to Pofuduk, this page is updated before that feature ships. The date at the top shows the last update.
